ESTABLISHED 2014

M.H General Contracting is based in Al Ain, with construction delivery across Al Ain and Abu Dhabi.

Since 2014, our portfolio has grown to 150+ completed and ongoing projects, developing experience across educational, commercial, industrial and residential construction. Our role is practical and accountable: to execute the works, organise the site and maintain coordination with the project parties.

Experienced management works alongside engineers, foremen and construction teams to turn approved requirements into a clear sequence of work. Resource planning, trade coordination, supervision and follow-up support the delivery of each assigned scope.
